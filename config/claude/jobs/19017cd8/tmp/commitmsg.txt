one vocabulary, and every pane speaks it

Six surfaces had each drawn their own version of the same controls, so the app
looked like six apps sharing a grid. The tokens are two tiers now: primitives
that nothing outside `base.css` may name, and a semantic map that is the only
thing a component is allowed to read. Density is one block to re-point rather
than a sweep through every file, and `styles/tokens.test.ts` fails the build if
a component reaches past the map for a raw hex, a raw space or a raw z-index.

The dark map is a re-map and not an inversion. Elevation is carried by surface
lightness, because a drop shadow on a dark ground is invisible by construction,
and the accent is lightened and desaturated, because a saturated teal on a dark
ground blooms. Neither map is the OS's decision: the stored `pref` is the
authority and `data-theme` is stamped before paint.

`src/ui/` is what stops the six-apps problem coming back. `.icon-btn`, `.field`,
`.select`, `.chip` and `.stat` are each one treatment for a job that had four
private implementations. `.field` took its geometry verbatim from the best of
the existing inputs rather than inventing a seventh, so adopting it was not a
visual change for the two fields that already looked right.

Then the five places the app contradicted itself, all of them found by driving
it rather than by reading it:

- A note's body sizes to its content. It was a permanently expanded three-row
  textarea, so three notes filled the pane and the placeholder "Write in
  Markdown…" repeated down it — a list of notes that could not be read as a
  list. No edit mode was added to fix it: it is the same always-editable field,
  and `field-sizing: content` with `rows={1}` means an empty note is one line
  and a written one is as tall as it needs, capped at eight so a single long
  note cannot eat the pane. Chromium-only, which this app is; the `rows={1}`
  fallback is a one-line field at the same resting height rather than nothing.

- The journal offered one action twice — a composer, and directly under it a
  command that did the same job. The composer is the primary action and the
  duplicate is gone. An entry's body is prose now, in a prose face at
  `--measure-prose`, instead of monospace running the width of a 2x2 pane.

- The inspector is contained by the pane that opens it. It was a column in the
  pane's flow with nothing capping it, and the pane is `overflow: hidden` — so
  at 1x1 its own "Add link" button was below the fold with no way to scroll to
  it. The panel had been unusable at the size it is most often opened at.

- A habit's cadence is a dressed control rather than a bare `<select>`, which
  was the loudest inconsistency on the screen. The chevron is a sibling span
  and not a `background-image`: a data URI is a separate document, resolves
  neither `currentcolor` nor a custom property, and would have needed a literal
  hex that could not follow the theme. A streak reads as a figure with its unit
  instead of a naked digit that says nothing about what it counts.

- A day that holds something now looks different from a day that does not. The
  calendar's entries were a hairline you had to know was there.

`Calendar.tsx` gained `data-type` and not one entity type name. A module
colours its own chip from its own file, which is the only way per-type
appearance can exist without the generic surface learning what a todo is —
`views/genericity.test.ts` asserts that against the source so it cannot
regress quietly.

Verified: `check` clean, 382 unit tests, 56 Playwright tests across desktop and
mobile viewports, and every pane driven by hand in both themes.
