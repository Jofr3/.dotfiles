## Medieval Bravas

##### A story that became a legend.

Some dishes are created to accompany a meal, while others become part of a restaurant's history. Our Medieval Bravas are one of those that don't go unnoticed.

A unique recipe, a combination of flavors and our own way of understanding good patatas bravas that, over time, have become one of the great symbols of Xinvic.

The recognition came in 2024, when they were chosen as the best patatas bravas in Barcelona. And far from being just a memory, we achieved it again in 2025, proving that a good recipe isn't a matter of luck, but of passion, quality ingredients, and a strong desire to do things right.

Today, our Medieval Bravas are part of the essence of Xinvic. A dish that speaks of us, of our way of cooking, and of that constant quest to transform something as simple as bravas into an experience you'll want to repeat.

**Dos años. Dos victorias. Una misma pasión.**

## Nuestra Filosofía

##### Cocinar con cuidado. Servir con cariño.

En Xinvic apostamos por una cocina de calidad, creativa y elaborada con esmero. Nos gusta cuidar cada detalle, desde la elección de los ingredientes hasta el momento en que el plato llega a la mesa.

Pero para nosotros, la gastronomía no termina en la cocina. El ambiente, el servicio, la compañía y la forma en que te sientes cuando estás con nosotros también forman parte de la experiencia. **Porque queremos que vengas a comer, pero sobre todo que vengas a disfrutar.**
